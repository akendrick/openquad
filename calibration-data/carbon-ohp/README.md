# Digital negatives for carbon transfer: coated office transparency film

Canon PIXMA MG3600 printing on inkjet-coated office overhead film (no surfactant yet: Tween 20 not
arrived, so ink beads/reticulates), carbon tissue at the usual exposure. Printed from GIMP as is.
Print dialog media setting: not recorded yet.

## Result, 23 Oct 2026

| Colour | Full patch L\* | Blocks fully | Range (L\*) | Usable steps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| black | 82.8 | no | 55.8 | 8/11 |
| green | 92.4 | yes | 65.3 | **9/11** |
| orange | **94.0** | yes | 64.7 | 9/11 |
| yellow | 93.1 | yes | 64.5 | 8/11 |
| red | 90.2 | no (just) | 65.4 | 9/11 |
| olive | 92.1 | yes | 67.9 | 7/11 |

- **Black blocks the least UV** on this film; the coloured inks (yellow dye) block more. The black
  safe edge leaked (L\* 87.9), so safe edges are now printed in the negative's colour.
- Process white (orange) is only 4.8 L\* below bare paper: tissue stain, not over-exposure.
- Best: **green** (9 usable steps over 65 L\*), orange a close second and the strongest blocker.
- Spotting and ink reticulation (no Tween 20 yet) are visible; patch medians ignore most of it.
